# Village: Gadwick

Gadwick is contained in the towers and expansive dungeon and crypts of the once-grand Castle Gadwick. It has a Prosperity of 0 and the following institutions and their stewards:

- 1st-Level Alchemist: Brune
- 1st-Level Auction House: Lili
- 2nd-Level Barracks: Cormal
- 2nd-Level Blacksmith: Deirdre
- 1st-Level Bookseller: Rion
- 1st-Level Enchanter: Isaac
- 3rd-Level General Store: Sorcha
- 3rd-Level Stables: Anna
- 1st-Level Temple: Mackle
- 1st-Level Inn: Duna
- 1st-Level Crypt: Oda. Boons available: Greed (1), Rescue (1), Vitality (1).
